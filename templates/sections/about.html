About Our Association

Connecting JUians of Gaibandha

JUians of Gaibandha is a community platform for students, alumni, teachers and employees of Jahangirnagar University who have connections with Gaibandha District.

Our goal is to build a strong network among JUians, preserve our community connection and make important information easily accessible through a centralized digital platform.
